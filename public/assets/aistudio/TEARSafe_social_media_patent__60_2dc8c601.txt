TITLE OF THE INVENTION:
SYSTEMS AND METHODS FOR BIOMETRICALLY WATERMARKED SOCIAL STREAMING, VEHICULAR TELEMETRIC BROADCASTING, COLLABORATIVE GROUP MEDIA HUBS, AND SENSOR-TRIGGERED EMERGENCY DISPATCH

================================================================================
1. TECHNICAL FIELD & INVENTIVE OBJECTIVE
================================================================================
The present provisional application describes novel computer-implemented architectures, cryptographic protocols, and hardware integrations for a tamper-resistant social media, video streaming, and community safety network designated as "TearSafe Social" and the "T.E.A.R.S. Channel". The invention prevents digital impersonation (deepfakes), synchronizes mobile video broadcasts with physical vehicle telematics (OBD-II/CAN-bus), provides high-performance group watchrooms, and dynamically converts active social broadcasts into high-priority emergency rescue feeds.

================================================================================
2. DETAILED TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & EMBODIMENTS
================================================================================
A. Hardware-Anchored Biometric Content Watermarking:
- Dual-spectrum infrared and optical camera capture extracting 468 3D facial landmark vertices.
- Dermal micro-vascular pulse wave extraction via remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) confirming living human origin.
- Secure Enclave cryptographic signing of video slice frames with geographic coordinates, preventing synthetic AI manipulation.

B. In-Transit Vehicular Telemetry Integration:
- Direct hardware pairing with vehicle On-Board Diagnostics (OBD-II) and internal IMU sensors.
- Synchronized heads-up display rendering speed, acceleration G-forces, vehicle battery/fuel levels, and cabin acoustics onto the media stream.

C. Low-Latency Group Watchrooms & Corporate Training:
- Synchronized multi-user WebRTC playback clusters with sub-50ms playback alignment.
- Biometric attendance verification continuously verifying participant identity for corporate compliance, enterprise training, and licensed educational courses.

D. Red-Alert Biometric Distress Escalation:
- Automated sensor anomaly detection (acoustic gunshots, screaming, abnormal pulse >140 BPM, sudden deceleration >3.5 G).
- Automatic bifurcation into an obscured public social feed and a secure, encrypted emergency evidence tunnel transmitting raw footage and GPS coordinates directly to 911 / Public Safety Answering Points (PSAP).

E. Decentralized Creator Economy with Zero-Slippage Biometric Micro-Tips:
- Biometrically authorized instant tip settlements crediting 90% net revenue to creator wallets without third-party intermediary delays.

================================================================================
3. INFORMAL CLAIMS & INVENTIVE SCOPE
================================================================================
- A mobile device configured to capture video while simultaneously sampling photoplethysmography pulse data to verify biological origin and attach an unforgeable hardware signature.
- A method of overlaying real-time vehicle telemetric telemetry onto a social live stream using OBD-II/BLE data links.
- A peer-to-peer caching method enabling offline video queueing across a local vehicular mesh during cellular dead zones.
- An emergency escalation method that splits an active public live stream into a private evidentiary law enforcement stream upon detecting physical stress markers.
